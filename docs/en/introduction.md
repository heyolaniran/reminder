Calendrian is a powerful engine designed to help you reach your audience directly on their lock screens through calendar notifications. 

By integrating Calendrian into your own systems, you can automate event reminders, product launches, or any time-sensitive outreach with extremely high visibility.

## Key Features

- **Direct Visibility**: Bypass email inboxes and spam filters.
- **Local-First Privacy**: Use cryptographic keys and local storage to manage identity.
- **Scheduling**: Send notifications immediately or schedule them for the future.
- **Engagement Insights**: Track who accepted, declined, or is tentatively coming to your event.

## Getting Started

To start using the Calendrian API, you'll need:
1. A **Master Access Key** (obtained by supporting the project on the dashboard).
2. A **Google Refresh Token** (if you want to send reminders from your own account).
